  1. Share the context / 分享背景
    Company, audience, objectives, preferred timing and budget range with currency.
    公司、受众、目标、预计时间及含币种的预算范围。
  2. Explore the fit / 讨论适合的方向
    Relevant brands, content formats and project roles.
    适合的品牌、内容形式和各方负责范围。
  3. Agree the scope / 确认范围
    Deliverables, credits, approvals and commercial terms before proceeding.
    开始前共同确认交付内容、署名、审批流程和商业条款。

Our journey / 发展历程

2023 — Red Point established in Kuching, Sarawak, with a purpose to combine traditional media judgment with new media expression.
Red Point 于砂拉越古晋创立，取传统媒体的判断与责任，融合新媒体的速度与表达。

2024–2025 — The team grew from three people to fifteen, expanding into Chinese and English media, creative production, social content, automotive and technology.
团队从 3 人成长至 15 人，业务延伸至华文与英文资讯、创意制作、社交内容，以及汽车与科技领域。

2026 — Red Point continues to deepen its Kuching and Sarawak base while developing business beyond its home market.
持续深耕古晋与砂拉越，同时积极发展其他地区的业务、客户与合作网络。

2027 TARGET — Grow across Malaysia and carry the Red Point brand story forward.
目标将业务发展至全马，继续发扬 Red Point 的品牌故事。
